Approximate manual digitization of scalar Rayleigh constraints from Figure 1 of Barducci et al., JHEP 06 (2025) 171 (arXiv:2501.09073).
These are suitable for quick overlays, not publication-grade extraction.
For a paper figure, re-digitize from the PDF with WebPlotDigitizer.

The four *_FERMI_single_line / *_FERMI_double_line files were removed: Figure 1
carries a single Fermi curve, so a single/double split has no source in it.
